I used to read books. Bachon ki stories to mein abhi bhi parh loon. Good ones tho. not the shitty ones. Yes, there are shitty children's books. I haven't read any new children's books but I can see from kids tv shows and shit is how shitty they're becoming. Hamare bachpan k shows aren't just nostalgia, yes there is nostalgia but there was more to them. They were actually well done kids shows. Now it's just half baked embarrassments of thoughts colorized and fed to the new kids. They don't touch mattering subjects; they fear to tread already known waters. They started sugarcoating shit so long ago that now they just sell sugar. Khair, I'm going off on a tangent.

My thoughts don't come as written in the "journal". I just write the final product of it. I don't write the whole thought that comes. They happen as a conversation. Someone says something to me, sometimes in a certain context. Sometimes I'm not actually part of the conversation happening. Other people talk and I just listen. One things lead to another and at a certain point I feel like writing something down and so I do.
