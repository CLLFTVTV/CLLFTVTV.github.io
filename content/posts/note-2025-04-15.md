I feel like there might be someone living in the attic. I mean, I've been up there a few times and I haven't found anyone. But every now and then, I hear a sound from up there, or something isn't where I left it, or just a general air about the room that might suggest co-occupancy.

---

I consider my body to be a vessel and that I (whatever that means) am occupying or being channeled through this vessel. However, there are times when it is partially or completely occupied by an other whatever. It's like the body stops experiencing it's "actual" environment and temporarily experiences an alternate one. This alternate environment is more often than not a fictionalized version of the body's usual environments, one that does not, but possibly may, exist. As for the times when that is not the case, I have no thoughts to capture that essence. Furthermore, I only say this because I've noticed the aftermath of it, the body 'feels different'. It's like the body was something else a moment ago. It's like the body is pretending to be my body, like it feigns loyalty, chooses to seem not-conscious, though I'm convinced that that is not the case. Importantly, I have never noticed myself "leaving" and later "re-entering" my body. I used to wave off the aftermath as imaginative musing. But once upon a time, out of the corner of my eye, I happened to notice my body in the act. Like it was addressing this alternate environment. Naturally, I decided to put up hidden cameras to catch myself in the act and lo and behold! "Suli is talking to himself". _Maybe_ that's why some thoughts seem pre-developed when conceived while others pick themselves up from where I left them. I guess I've said too much of it for now. Perhaps I should wait for more such occurrences.
