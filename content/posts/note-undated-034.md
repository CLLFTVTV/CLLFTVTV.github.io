Back when I was soft and shy,
One night, when I was woke.
I met a cooler, calmer guy-
Inside a puff of smoke.

And so I smoked,
and smoked,
And found, 
Without a smoke or three,
I wasn't fun to be around,
Nor was I meant to be.

By now I don't know if I can quit,
Or even if I should.
Maybe life will not be shit,
It might just turn for good.

I might just be a happier guy,
But, of that, I'm not so sure.
So, I close my eyes and sadly sigh,

And then, I light one more.
