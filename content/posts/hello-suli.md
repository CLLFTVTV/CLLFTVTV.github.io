This is the first note. Everything below the front matter (the block fenced
by `---`) is just plain **Markdown**, so you can write the way you'd write
anywhere else.

A few quick things you can use in a note:

- **bold** and *italic*
- `inline code`
- > blockquotes for things you're quoting

Notes are sorted newest-first automatically, so the most recent thing you
write always sits at the top of the homepage.
