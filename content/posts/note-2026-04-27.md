Used to love playing GTA: VC as a kid. Either that or I loved listening to music. At least I thought I loved listening to music but I guess I never really knew what music was capable of. I'd had heard a lot of songs; on the radio, in movies, from family. And I didn't really notice it at the time but they all had a very similar emotional range. Love, relationships, happiness, sadness, the use. And I used to think that yeah this music is speaking to me, how it voices things I voice too. But there was more to it than that; or maybe more to me, i guess. 

TL:DR heard Em's Cleaning Out My Closet and Superman in GTA: VC. His expression of aggression, rage and anger spoke to me deeply. Could never have imagined that that was possible with music.
