when i was in the third grade (~9 y/o), we were taught what a limerick was and we had to write one in class. For the life of me I couldn't come up with something to write. Class ended and next was either recess or a sports period but I wasn't allowed to go because I hadn't written one. So there I was, in class, alone, with one wall of floor-to-ceiling windows looking out to the play ground where the rest of the class was playing. Now, I was never much of a sports kid, but I liked being outside, being active, using my body, walking around, being out in the open, looking at the sky et cetera, so ofc I wanted out. But, I still didn't have anything to write. So, I thought I could start going thru the other kids' bags and look at their writings. Now, I don't remember how many I checked and what I read, but I do remember one which which went like something something mice and we ate rice with slice. like holy shit that isn't even a proper limerick, his first two lines rhymed and the third, fourth and fifth rhymed. I thought to myself well shit if THAT's the threshold then my bullshit would suffice. so i just wrote one and took my note book and went out to the teacher who, along with one other teacher, was sitting at a desk looking towards the play ground. I handed her my note book, waiting for her approval when the other teacher asks her who's this?, referring to me. my teacher answered (I have another story with this same teacher about synonyms) that oh this is one of my students and as an introduction she read out the limerick I wrote. Me, seeing that the limerick was obviously approved, decided to be cocky and ask if I'm allowed to go and she said yes I am. Fin. *close curtains*.
