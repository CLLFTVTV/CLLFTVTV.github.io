Old Officer's Colony Park
Night time
*** on my bunk bed in the park
I was getting ice cream from vendor. Another guy walked up to him before me and requested a certain brand. I asked for two of the same. But the vendor misunderstood me and gave me a burger. I was like "burger nahi maanga, ice cream chahiye hai". He was like, "phir ye burger kis ne maanga hai?". "Mein ne nai", me.
