Sanfransokyo midnight drone battle

I was walking out of the door late at night for some reason. Outside was a dark sky, and the cars and houses on the skew street were illuminated by a blue moon somewhere to the right, where the street was descending.
I felt the steps and pavement under my bare feet as I walked past them into the road to hug the seemingly female white long furred cat that was laying (lieing? Lying? Laying?) in the middle of the street. I sat down facing the down side (yeah) with my legs crossed, picked her up and put her in my lap and cradled it as if it were a human baby. All the virtual lines of car tops, house roofs, street lights and city planning converged to a great stadium which lit up the entire city scape. A sort of competition was taking place but I've never had the slightest inclination to pay attention when sports are input in my brain. Though I did marvel at the prettiness of the drone battle, like space ships in a starry sky.
